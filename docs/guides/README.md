# Guides

These guides explain how to set up, explore, present, and operate the current
demo. Choose a guide based on the task you need to complete; you do not need to
read them in file-name order.

If Fabric terminology is new to you, keep the
[plain-language glossary](glossary.md) open while you work.

## Start or deploy the demo

| Guide | Best for | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Getting started](getting-started.md) | First-time operators and entry-level developers | Prerequisites, profile choice, configuration, deployment, first data, and first validation. |
| [Workspace and profile inventory](workspace-inventory.md) | Operators comparing deployment sizes | Exact capabilities, item counts, machine-learning tiers, folders, and support boundaries for `core`, `standard`, and `full-demo`. |
| [Deployment](deployment.md) | Operators updating or recovering a workspace | Preflight, Terraform, publication order, generated evidence, safe reruns, exact-run recovery, and troubleshooting. |

## Understand or present the demo

| Guide | Best for | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Use cases](use-cases.md) | Business users and solution owners | Retail questions the demo supports, current maturity, and optional ideas. |
| [Deployed walkthrough](deployed-walkthrough.md) | Business analysts and mixed audiences | A short workspace orientation and links to focused tours. |
| [Data platform walkthrough](deployed-data-platform.md) | Data and analytics teams | Pipelines, notebooks, historical tables, and optional live events. |
| [Analytics and AI walkthrough](deployed-analytics-ai.md) | Business and analytics teams | Ontology, Data Agents, machine-learning signals, and Power BI. |
| [Presenter demo](demo-script.md) | Presenters running a 15-20 minute demo | A complete talk track with evidence and qualification guidance. |
| [Presenter journeys](presenter-journeys.md) | Presenters running a focused 5-7 minute story | Repeatable store operations, merchandising, and executive scenarios. |
